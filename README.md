SKYRUNNER: Dash Through the Void
SKYRUNNER is a high-octane, pixel-punk endless runner designed for retro-arcade enthusiasts and competitive players alike. Set against a vibrant, neon-drenched sky, you must test your reflexes as you leap, duck, and weave through a fast-paced obstacle course that speeds up with every second.

Key Features:
🏃 Multiple Characters: Choose your favorite runner, each bringing their own vibe to the rooftop dash , become a runner make your own character .

⏱️ Fast-Paced Gameplay: Master simple controls—jump over high hazards, duck under low barriers, and react fast to survive.

🏆 Global Public Leaderboard: Sign in to save your high scores, compare your runs with the community, and climb your way to the top spot!

Whether you’re looking to kill a few minutes or dominate the leaderboards, test your skills now.
